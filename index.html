PhD candidate, Materials Science, McGill University (NanoFactory group)

I am a PhD researcher in materials science with expertise in atomistic and mesoscale simulations, including density functional theory and molecular dynamics. My work focuses on thermodynamics, kinetics, interfaces, and defects in low-dimensional materials for energy and electronic applications. I translate fundamental insights from physics-based modeling into practical solutions for energy systems, sensors, and next-generation electronics.
